One line, at most 200 characters, in which a robot vacuum explains why it missed a spot:

> I skipped that spot deliberately: my sensors logged the dust bunny as a tenant with seniority, and I don't evict anyone older than my firmware.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes that line deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`.
